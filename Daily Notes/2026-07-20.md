Before doing something that requires confidence do some breathing exercises, inhaling increases heart rate whereas exhaling lowers it typically so exhaling 2x as long as inhaling can help calm you down. In addition stare at one spot for 30 seconds to still your eyes from darting around nervously.

Slow down your speech
Build a habit of pausing for ~2 seconds before speaking
Let people speak at you
First impressions are incredibly important

Lower your voice slightly but in tone and volume when their voice gets higher / excited so that they naturally quiet down to listen to you and don’t help escalate.

Use when/then conditional structure for promises you know you can keep

Let a decision stand and don’t over explain after having given an answer
When dealing with children: stop asking
Are we ready to clean up?" makes compliance feel optional.  A direct statement like "It is time to clean up" is better. Think about what you are going to say before you say it as well. They have a 1000mile a minute pace.

Be more comfortable with pauses
Have whole expressions planned before you say them so that you aren’t just speaking slowly but like a robot building a sentence word by word
